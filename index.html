What Happens To Your Video

Every step below is a real FFmpeg operation — you can see the exact commands in the terminal log.

01
🧬

Metadata Stripping

FFmpeg command: -map_metadata -1

Removes all embedded EXIF headers — camera model, GPS coordinates, device serial number, creation timestamps, encoder software info.

02
🎛️

Video Re-Encoding

FFmpeg command: -c:v libx264 -crf [N] -vf unsharp

Full x264 re-encode with unsharp filter alters the pixel-level data while preserving visual quality you can see.

03
🔊

Audio Re-Encoding

FFmpeg command: -c:a aac -af atempo=0.999

Re-encodes to fresh AAC with a 0.1% imperceptible tempo micro-shift that alters the acoustic waveform without any audible change.

Frequently Asked Questions

🔍

⚙️ Technical

Is this processing done on my computer or a server?▼

100% on your computer. FFmpeg.wasm is a WebAssembly version of FFmpeg that runs inside your browser. Your video file never leaves your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Why does the tool take 30-60 seconds to start first time?▼

FFmpeg.wasm needs to download the WASM core (~25 MB) on first load. After that, your browser caches it and it loads instantly every time. This is a one-time download.

What metadata tags are removed exactly?▼

FFmpeg -map_metadata -1 removes: Title, Comment, Artist, Album, GPS Coordinates, Camera Make & Model, Device Serial Number, Creation Date & Time, Encoder Software name, Copyright tag, and all other embedded EXIF/ID3/MP4 box metadata.

Does re-encoding reduce video quality?▼

At CRF 18-22, the output is visually identical to the original. CRF 23 (default/balanced) produces near-lossless output. Only at CRF 26+ will you notice slight compression. Use the quality slider to control this.

📋 Usage

Which video formats are supported?▼

Input: MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WEBM, FLV, M4V. Output: MP4 (H.264 + AAC) or WebM (VP8). MP4 is recommended for maximum compatibility.

Is there a file size limit?▼

Free trial: 50 MB. Starter plan: 500 MB. Pro Creator: 2 GB. Enterprise: Unlimited. Large files may take several minutes depending on your CPU speed.
